Mobile Development

Mobile Apps Design & Development for iOS & Android

CRM & DMP Integration

Stress Testing, Security & Maintenance

 

As a full-fledged digital agency also acting as a software development company, we have evolved to become a top mobile app development company in Egypt, accommodating the rising need of mobile-first products & services from its early stages until today.

Our award-winning mobile applications have brought many brands to light by showcasing their products and services through trendy designs and agile development practices for both iOS and Android. Our passionate team employs the latest technologies and trends in mobile design and development to deliver the best applications while maintaining security protocols and constantly following up to guarantee your mobile app’s operability and brand’s satisfaction.

Catering to a multitude of clients across many sectors, our mandate as one of the best app development companies in Egypt is to always place the user at the forefront of a brand’s mobile presence by creating fluid experiences that allows users to explore and discover the mobile application’s offering while keeping it fast, intuitive, and easy to use. Our growing list of satisfied clients for whom we delivered mobile applications includes the Ministry of Investment & International Cooperation (currently renamed to Ministry of International Cooperation), where we created the official companion app of the Investment For Africa Forum in 2018 and 2019, which provided a comprehensive mobile app exclusively for the event that enabled African heads of states, government leaders, prominent regional & international investors and financial institutions to connect, communicate and collaborate with each other during the third and fourth iteration of the Pan-African platform in Egypt.

In the automotive world, we created several mobile apps for our clients living in the fast lane, such as the exceptional BMW Grand Class app which caters to BMW Egypt’s finest clientele; the owners of the 6, 7 and 8 Series models. We are also proud to have delivered trendy mobile apps to each of Ford, Mitsubishi, Volkswagen, and Pirelli in Egypt.

Additional mobile apps by Icon Creations include an all-inclusive app that delivers news, updates and general information on Egypt’s leading football club Al Ahly SC, an award-winning app for SCIB Paints that claimed the Best Designed Mobile App Award 2018, and the Egyptian Ministry of Emigration’s first-ever mobile app that aims to bridge connections between the Ministry, Egyptian nationals and expatriates, Kalem Masr.

Discover our work, explore our client portfolio, or get in touch to request a quotation or ask us your questions about mobile application development.
