As one of the top social media agencies in Egypt today that started off as one of the advertising agencies in Egypt, our social media services are full-fledged; from platform and application development, community management, moderation and CMS management to content creation, sentiment analysis and full social campaigns, we provide out-of-the-box solutions that come together with our passion for creativity and hunger for innovation. Through these services, we are able to increase communication & maximize engagement with your target audience in order to reflect your business values and grow brand loyalty.

Today, Icon Creations has established itself as a top digital marketing and social media agency in Egypt, evolving its comprehensive services to accommodate all industries across Egypt and the Middle East. Our creative drive empowered by our passion for innovation has led us to deliver several social media campaigns to a variety of clients in different sectors over the years, including but not limited to: governmental institutions such as the Central Bank of Egypt, the Ministry of International Cooperation (formerly known as the Ministry of Investment & International Cooperation) and the Egyptian Ministry of Emigration; leading automotive brands such as BMW, Ferrari, Mitsubishi, MINI, Maserati, Jaguar, Pirelli, Castrol and Ford; top FMCG brands such as PepsiCo, Nestlé, Coca-Cola Family Program, McDonalds, Americana Group, KFC Arabia, and Juhayna; financial institutions such as World Bank Group, Crédit Agricole, Arab African International Bank, National Bank of Egypt, and Emirates NBD; cosmetic and makeup brands such as Nivea, L'Oréal, Maybelline, Vichy Laboratories, Kérastase, La Roche-Posay, Lancôme, and Garnier; Real estate developers such as Memaar AlMorshedy, Uptown Cairo, Emaar Misr, Iwan Developments, ElTayebi Developments, Gates Developments, Mountain View, and Real Mark, in addition to a variety of leading local, regional and global organizations such as UN Women, Al Ahly FC, ENOC, Vodafone, Nokia, Allianz, Hilton, Hyatt, Lufthansa, Transmar, Phillips, and SOUQ.
