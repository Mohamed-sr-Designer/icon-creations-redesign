MINI Egypt

Brief:

We celebrated the start of 2019 by documenting MINI Egypt's exciting showroom, showcasing their top models such as the MINI 3 Door, MINI 5 Door, MINI Cabrio and MINI Countryman.

In 2020, we created a 3-part video campaign to promote the MINI Countryman in Egypt, as part of a global marketing competition for which our creative submission for MINI Egypt won the MINI Marketing Award for letting photographer Abdel Rahman Koree explore Egypt with the Countryman.
