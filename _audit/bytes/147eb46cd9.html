Arab African International Bank

Brief:

As part of our complete coverage of the Arab African International Bank's sponsorship series for the El Gouna Squash Open 2018, we provided a variety of informative and recap-oriented videos throughout the tournament. From the history of Squash to participant profiles, the AAIB’s “Power of 7” is still a hugely popular campaign that has been followed by fans of the sport across Egypt and the Arab world.

The Semi Finals

El Gouna International Squash Open 2018

Competition further intensified at the semi finals stage, where world no. 1 Nour El Sherbini - on her return to the site of her last World Championship win - gave a powerful performance and won over Laura Massaro 3 - 1.

The Finals
