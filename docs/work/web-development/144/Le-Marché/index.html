Brief:

Le Marché is the largest full-fledged furniture, material and home accessories festival in the MENA region that brings together the industry’s top-notch suppliers, designers, manufacturers, traders, businessmen and producers, while catering to thousands of consumers and furniture enthusiasts.

With over three decades of experience, Le Marché has become the leading furniture hub in Egypt, welcoming more than 85,000 visitors per round over a period of 4 days across an exhibition area of 32,000 m² and displaying over 300 international and local brands.
