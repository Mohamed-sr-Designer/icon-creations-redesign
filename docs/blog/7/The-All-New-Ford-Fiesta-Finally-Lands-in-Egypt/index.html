The All-New Ford Fiesta Finally Lands in Egypt

Driving around Cairo streets has never been an easy task. Whether it’s the traffic, noise, pollution, or the rollercoaster-like roads, it’s difficult to find a car that tackles all these obstacles. But one certain vehicle strikes a wonderful balance between comfort, style, and speed. Ladies and gentlemen, meet the all-new Ford Fiesta.

Ford has been a staple in European and American markets, but in recent years, the Egyptian market has seen the huge spread of the Ford Focus, proving how reliable Ford cars are. The Fiesta, the smaller sister, has been the most popular hatchback for God knows how long in Europe, and it’s about time Egyptians and Cairenes realized how suitable it is for Cairo roads.
