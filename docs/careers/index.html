Senior Account Manager / Account Manager

Job Title: Senior Account Manager

The Senior Account Manager is responsible for leading client relationships, ensuring smooth project delivery, driving business growth, and coordinating internal teams to meet client expectations. They act as the strategic bridge between the client and the agency, delivering outstanding service, building trust, and expanding account opportunities.

Key Responsibilities:

Client Relationship Management

  • - Serve as the primary point of contact for key clients.
    - Build strong, long-term relationships based on trust, performance, and understanding.
    - Manage daily communication, meetings, reporting, and strategic updates in a timely manner.

Project & Campaign Management

  • - Oversee the planning, execution, and delivery of campaigns and projects.
    - Develop detailed project timelines, scopes, and deliverable lists.
    - Coordinate between internal teams (creative, media, digital, production) and external suppliers. Ensure all projects meet deadlines, budgets, and client expectations.

Strategic Planning & Growth

  • - Understand client business goals, industries, and challenges.
    - Identify new opportunities for upselling or cross-selling agency services.
    - Collaborate on proposals, pitches, and presentations for business growth.
    - Support in setting KPIs, tracking results, and optimizing account performance.

Financial Management

  • - Prepare and manage client budgets, billing, and financial forecasting.
    - Ensure profitable account management by controlling scopes and costs.
    - Track and approve all work in line with contract agreements.

Reporting & Analytics

  • - Provide clients with regular performance reports, campaign summaries, and strategic insights.
    - Analyze campaign results to identify improvements and best practices.

Interested applicants should send their CVs to careers@icon-creations.com and mention the position in the subject line of the email.
